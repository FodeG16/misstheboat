Beating the crowds

Local intel

  • Deià village 10 min north: Robert Graves' house (La Casa de Robert Graves) open to visitors, extraordinary terraced gardens, far fewer tourists
  • Tramuntana mountain road (Ma-10): drive it yourself rather than on a coach — viewpoints with zero cruise buses
  • Fornalutx village beyond Sóller: arguably the most beautiful village in Spain, almost no coaches go there
