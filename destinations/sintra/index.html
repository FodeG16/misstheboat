What the crowds actually look like

Extraordinary concentration of Romantic architecture in a small area. Genuinely worth visiting — the challenge is the crowds.

Day-trip from Lisbon (40 min by train) that has become severely overcrowded. Pena Palace queues reach 2–3 hours in summer. The town centre and castle approaches are gridlocked by coaches by 11am.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Take the first train from Lisbon (Rossio station, departs ~7am). Palace queues are manageable before 9am. Leave by 1pm.

Alternative: Óbidos — 1hr from Lisbon. Medieval walled village, fraction of the visitors, excellent ginja cherry liqueur.
