What the crowds actually look like

Long cruise excursion from Casablanca (2.5hrs each way). Djemaa el-Fna square and souks are intense even without cruise groups — with them it's overwhelming midday.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Souks before 10am or after 5pm. The Saadian Tombs and Bahia Palace are less crowded than Djemaa el-Fna at any hour.

Local intel

  • Bahia Palace at 8:30am opening — cruise groups don't reach it until 11am
  • Mellah (Jewish Quarter) for a quiet hour away from the souks frenzy
  • Café des Épices in the spice market has rooftop views with minimal tourist pressure
  • Jardin Majorelle (Yves Saint Laurent) requires advance booking — worth it, usually manageable
  • Dinner at a small riad restaurant not on a tourist menu — your riad will know the right places
