What the crowds actually look like

Appears on some Civitavecchia cruise menus but far less frequently than Rome. Manageable even on port days.

Local intel

  • Orvieto Underground (tunnels and caves beneath the city) — guided tour, extraordinary, cruise groups rarely book it
  • Cathedral facade is one of the finest Gothic works in Italy — at its best in morning light before crowds
  • Pozzo di San Patrizio (St Patrick's Well) — double-helix Renaissance well, engineering marvel, rarely crowded
  • Cantina Foresi wine bar in Piazza del Duomo — Orvieto Classico at the source, outdoor tables facing the cathedral
  • Drive down to the old river town (Orvieto Scalo) for a local lunch — no tourists, trattoria prices
