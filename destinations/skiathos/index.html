What the crowds actually look like

Small island that can feel overwhelmed on busy port days. Koukounaries beach is the cruise group default.

Beating the crowds

Timing: The old town and Kastro (abandoned medieval capital) are rarely on cruise itineraries.

Local intel

  • Kastro (abandoned medieval capital): 45-min walk or boat, extraordinary ruins on a sea cliff, cruise groups never go
  • Lalaria beach (accessible only by boat): white pebbles, sculpted rock arch, dramatic — hire a small boat from the harbour
  • Monastery of Evangelistria: where the first Greek flag was raised (1807), 30-min walk from town, peaceful, almost no tourists
  • Morning coffee in the old port before the cruise tender brings the first wave — genuinely lovely
