What the crowds actually look like

Kyrenia's horseshoe harbour and Crusader castle are among the most photogenic in the eastern Mediterranean. In the Turkish-controlled north — requires crossing through Nicosia checkpoint or flying to Ercan.

Local intel

  • The Kyrenia castle and shipwreck museum (ancient Greek merchant ship) are extraordinary and rarely crowded
  • Bellapais Abbey above the town: Gothic ruins in a mountain village, Lawrence Durrell wrote about it, almost no visitors
  • Drive up to St Hilarion Castle: fairy-tale Crusader fortress in the mountains, commanding views, few tourists
