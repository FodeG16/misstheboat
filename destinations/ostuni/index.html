What the crowds actually look like

Dazzling whitewashed hilltop town. Growing in popularity but still manageable compared to more famous Pugliese towns.

Local intel

  • The centro storico is compact — walk through the back streets away from the main Corso Vittorio Emanuele
  • Cathedral terrace for the panorama over the olive grove valley toward the Adriatic
  • Masseria dining: several farm estates (masserias) outside town serve lunch — extraordinary setting, local food, no cruise groups
