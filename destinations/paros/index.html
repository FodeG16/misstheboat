What the crowds actually look like

Paros Town (Parikia) and Naoussa are the main cruise stops. Less overwhelmed than Mykonos but can get busy in peak summer. Growing in popularity.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Naoussa fishing village before 10am is genuinely beautiful.

Local intel

  • Naoussa village for dinner — prettier than Parikia, better restaurants, cruise groups less concentrated
  • Panagia Ekatontapiliani (Church of 100 Doors) — one of the oldest churches in Greece, free, rarely crowded
  • Antiparos island by ferry (10 min) — quieter, Cycladic village, stalactite cave
  • Golden Beach on the east coast for windsurfing — serious sport, not tourist infrastructure
