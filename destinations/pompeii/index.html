What the crowds actually look like

Naples cruise excursion staple. Coach convoys arrive from 10am — the main entrance and forum fill immediately. The site is large enough to escape groups if you move quickly past the obvious areas.

Beating the crowds

Timing: First entry slot (9am) is dramatically less crowded. Head immediately to the Villa of the Mysteries and amphitheater — cruise groups rarely reach them before noon.

Alternative: Herculaneum — 15 min from Naples, smaller but better-preserved, a fraction of the visitors.

Local intel

  • Herculaneum 20 min away is better preserved and far less crowded
  • Head immediately to the Garden of the Fugitives — cruise groups almost never reach it
  • The thermopolium (ancient fast food bar) is newly excavated and rarely crowded
  • Pompeii Archaeological Museum in Naples has the best artifacts removed from the site
