What the crowds actually look like

The last town before the Italian border. Far less visited than Monaco or Nice. Jean Cocteau museum, extraordinary gardens, and the best lemons in the world.

Local intel

  • Musée Jean Cocteau (Séverin Wunderman): extraordinary collection in a purpose-built museum, almost always quiet
  • Jardin Serre de la Madone: Lawrence Johnston's extraordinary hidden garden, limited visitor numbers, book ahead
  • The old town (Vieille Ville): Italian-influenced multicoloured houses, almost no tourists compared to Antibes or Villefranche
  • Menton lemons: buy from the market — they're genuinely different from any lemon you've had elsewhere
