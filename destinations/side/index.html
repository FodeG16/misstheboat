Local intel

  • Apollo temple at sunset: genuinely one of the most atmospheric ancient sites in Turkey — cruise groups long gone
  • Side Museum (converted Roman bath): extraordinary collection of local finds, rarely crowded
  • East beach beyond the Apollo temple: quieter than the main beach, local fish restaurants
