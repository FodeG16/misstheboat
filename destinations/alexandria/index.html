What the crowds actually look like

Most cruise excursions head straight to Cairo (3hrs away). Alexandria itself has the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Greco-Roman Museum, catacombs, and the Corniche seafront — all manageable without a guide.

Local intel

  • Bibliotheca Alexandrina: world-class modern library on the site of the ancient one, architecture is extraordinary
  • Catacombs of Kom el-Shoqafa: Greco-Roman burial chambers discovered by a donkey falling through the floor in 1900 — genuinely remarkable
  • Qaitbay Citadel built on the exact site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria — views of the harbour
  • Stanley Bridge neighbourhood: locals' seafront, seafood restaurants, entirely non-touristy
