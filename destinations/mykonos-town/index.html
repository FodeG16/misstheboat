What the crowds actually look like

Small Cycladic town with very limited capacity. Multiple ships in port simultaneously (common in summer) produce genuinely unpleasant crowding in the narrow streets.

Little Venice and the windmills area: severe day-tripper congestion from Athens ferries layered on cruise ship arrivals. The narrow lanes in the Chora are impassable at peak. The island is small — there is no escape within Mykonos Town.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Before 10am or after 6pm. The port-side cafes are peaceful at breakfast.

Alternative: Ano Mera — 8km inland, actual village, monastery, local tavernas. No cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Ano Mera village 8km away — monastery, local taverna, zero cruise presence
  • Fokos beach on the north coast: 15 min by scooter, no beach clubs, no cruise visitors
  • Little Venice at 7am for coffee with a view before the crowds arrive
  • Windmills area is manageable before 9am — after that, very crowded
