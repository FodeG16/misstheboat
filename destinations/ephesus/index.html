What the crowds actually look like

The single most visited ancient site in Turkey and one of the top Mediterranean cruise excursions from Kusadasi. The main street (Curetes Way) is completely dominated by cruise groups 10am–2pm.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Site opens at 8am. First 90 minutes before the cruise buses arrive is genuinely different. The Terrace Houses (ticketed separately) stay quieter throughout the day.

Alternative: Priene — 45 min south, equally atmospheric Hellenistic ruins on a hillside, virtually no cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Enter via the upper gate (Magnesia Gate) not the lower — cruise groups enter via the lower gate, you'll walk against the flow into a quieter section
  • Terrace Houses require a separate ticket and many cruise tours skip them — the preserved frescoes are extraordinary
  • Temple of Artemis (one of the Seven Wonders) is 2km from the main site — almost always empty
  • Sirince village 8km away: hillside wine village, no cruise buses, fruit wines, excellent lunch
