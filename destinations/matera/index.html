What the crowds actually look like

UNESCO Sassi cave dwellings — one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities on earth. Growing cruise excursion from Bari (1.5hrs). Still less crowded than most Italian UNESCO sites.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Evening is spectacular — the cave city lights up at dusk and cruise groups are long gone.

Local intel

  • Sasso Caveoso district is less visited than Sasso Barisano — same cave houses, fewer tour groups
  • Casa Noha (Fondo Ambiente Italiano): documentary screening about Matera's history in a restored cave house, rarely crowded
  • Walk down into the ravine (Gravina) below the Sassi: ancient cave churches carved in the cliff, almost no tourists go down
  • Matera at night: the cave city lit up is one of the most extraordinary sights in Italy — cruise groups are all gone by then
  • Rupestrian churches (rock-cut Byzantine churches with frescoes) dotted through the ravine — most tourists miss them entirely
