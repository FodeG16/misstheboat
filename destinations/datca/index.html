What the crowds actually look like

90-minute drive from Marmaris along a narrow peninsula. Ancient Knidos at the tip is rarely on cruise itineraries. Among the least-touristed coastal areas in the Aegean.

Local intel

  • Ancient Knidos at the peninsula tip: major Hellenistic city, dramatic setting on two harbours, almost no visitors
  • Datça's old town (Eski Datça): Ottoman stone houses, almond orchards, zero tourists
  • Fresh almonds and olive oil are the local speciality — buy direct from a producer not a tourist shop
