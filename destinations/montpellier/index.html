What the crowds actually look like

Major French university city with a well-preserved historic centre. Rarely on cruise excursion menus. Genuinely lively, cosmopolitan, local.

Local intel

  • Place de la Comédie: the main square is grand and lively — tram lines converge here, local life
  • Quartier Antigone: monumental neo-classical neighbourhood designed by Ricardo Bofill in the 1980s — extraordinary and bizarre, completely tourist-free
  • Marché du Lez on weekends: design market in a converted industrial space, Montpellier's most local cultural venue
  • Musée Fabre: one of France's great provincial art museums, barely known outside France — extraordinary collection
