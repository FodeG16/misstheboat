What the crowds actually look like

Antibes old town has extraordinary Picasso museum (in the castle where Picasso worked in 1946) and excellent market. Less crowded than Nice. Juan-les-Pins is the jazz festival venue.

Local intel

  • Musée Picasso (Château Grimaldi): the whole upper floor is Picasso's work from the 6 months he lived and worked here — extraordinary, rarely as crowded as it should be
  • Marché Provençal (morning market): one of the finest daily markets on the Côte d'Azur, local produce, under the ramparts
  • Ramparts walk at dusk: the old walls above the sea, stunning light, few tourists at that hour
  • Cap d'Antibes: the peninsula with Villa Eilenroc is walkable from the town, coastal path with wild flowers and sea views
