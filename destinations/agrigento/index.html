What the crowds actually look like

Long drive from Palermo (2hrs) limits cruise group size but they still fill the site. The Valley of Temples is vast enough to absorb crowds — the western temples are the least visited.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Site opens at 9am. Temple of Concordia before 10am is genuinely peaceful.

Local intel

  • Temple of Hephaestus and the western temples are a 20-min walk beyond the main area — almost no one goes
  • The site at dusk is extraordinary and cruise groups are long gone
  • Regional Archaeological Museum in town has the Ephebe of Agrigento — as good as anything in Sicily, rarely crowded
