What the crowds actually look like

Ibiza Town's Dalt Vila (upper old town) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site separate from the club scene. Cruise ships call at the harbour below. The old town is compact and fills quickly on port days.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Dalt Vila at 8am before the first ferries and cruise tenders start.

Alternative: Formentera island — 30 min ferry, car-free, extraordinary beaches, almost no cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Dalt Vila for sunset from the cathedral terrace — cruise groups leave by 5pm, then it's magical
  • Es Canar Wednesday hippy market — 45 min north, genuine counterculture institution since 1973
  • Formentera by ferry: the water is Caribbean-quality and there's almost no development
  • Cala d'Hort beach for the Es Vedrà rock view — difficult access keeps coach tours away
