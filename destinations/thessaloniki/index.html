What the crowds actually look like

Greece's second city and arguably its best food scene. Cruise ships visit but the city is large enough to absorb groups. The Roman Rotunda and Byzantine churches are the cruise focal points.

Beating the crowds

Timing: The Modiano and Kapani markets are best before noon. Ladadika nightlife district is purely local.

Local intel

  • Modiano and Kapani covered markets: two covered markets side by side — cheese, olives, spices, fresh fish — entirely local
  • Ladadika neighbourhood at night: former red-light district turned taverna strip, no tourists, extraordinary mezedes
  • Ano Poli (upper town): Ottoman-era neighbourhood above the city walls, traditional houses, panoramic views, virtually no cruise groups
  • Rotunda (early Christian monument): late-Roman mausoleum converted to mosque converted to church — extraordinary layers of history, rarely crowded
  • Thessaloniki is genuinely the best food city in Greece — eat bouyiourdi (baked feta with peppers) and loukoumades (honey fritters) here
