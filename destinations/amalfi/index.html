What the crowds actually look like

Naples cruise excursion staple. The coastal road (SS163) becomes severely congested on port days — buses from Naples add 40–60 min each way.

Both cruise excursion destination and heavy day-tripper traffic from Naples and Salerno by ferry and bus. The cathedral square and main street gridlock by late morning in peak season.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Early morning or after 5pm. The town is genuinely beautiful when the day-trippers leave.

Alternative: Ravello — 7km above Amalfi, no cruise buses, same coastline views, substantially better dining.

Local intel

  • Take the ferry rather than the road — avoids SS163 completely and adds a stunning coastal approach
  • Atrani (5 min walk from Amalfi) has one small piazza and almost no tourists
  • Villa Rufolo gardens in Ravello open at 9am — extraordinary views, minimal crowds before 10am
  • The Paper Museum (Museo della Carta) is free with a Campania Artecard and rarely visited
