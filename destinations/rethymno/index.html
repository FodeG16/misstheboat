Local intel

  • Fortezza (Venetian fortress) for panoramic views — cruise groups rarely climb up
  • Rimondi Fountain area at dusk — locals at kafenions, far more authentic than Chania harbour
  • Arkadi Monastery 25km inland — dramatic history (1866 revolt), extraordinary baroque church, almost no crowds
  • Cretaquarium east of Heraklion is worth knowing about — world-class Mediterranean marine exhibit, zero queue
