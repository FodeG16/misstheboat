What the crowds actually look like

France's oldest city (2,600 years) and largest Mediterranean port. MuCEM (Museum of European and Mediterranean Civilisations) and the Vieux-Port are the cruise stops. City is large and absorbs groups well.

Local intel

  • MuCEM: extraordinary architecture and collection — the walkway on top of the Fort Saint-Jean connected to the museum is one of the great architectural experiences in France
  • Cours Julien neighbourhood: street art, independent cafés, North African food, local life — completely off the cruise circuit
  • Bouillabaisse: the real thing requires a minimum of three types of fish and costs €50+. At Chez Michel or Restaurant Michel (not the tourist waterfront) — magnificent
  • Calanques by boat: the fjord-like inlets east of the city are extraordinary — join a boat trip from the Vieux-Port, it's what most tourists miss
  • Le Panier (old quarter): the oldest neighbourhood, steep streets, artisans, excellent for an hour before the crowds
