What the crowds actually look like

The monastery and dramatic serrated peaks are worth seeing. The experience depends entirely on avoiding the main plaza.

Day-trip from Barcelona (1.5h by rack railway). The cable car and rack railway platform are mobbed from 11am. On summer weekends the mountain receives 10,000+ visitors concentrated in 200m of walkway.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Take the Sant Joan funicular to the top rather than staying at the cable car arrival point. The mountain is large — go beyond and it's quiet.

Alternative: Take the Sant Joan funicular to the top: same mountain, fraction of the visitors. Tuesday or Wednesday morning in shoulder season.
