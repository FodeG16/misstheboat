What the crowds actually look like

Greece's largest Ionian island. Myrtos Beach is one of the most photographed in the world. Cruise calls are increasing. The island is large enough to escape groups.

Local intel

  • Myrtos Beach: arrive before 9am for the iconic view from above without the crowds, or hire a boat to approach from the sea
  • Melissani Lake cave: underground lake lit by a hole in the roof, extraordinary turquoise light — go at noon when the sun is directly overhead
  • Assos village: tiny peninsula village with Venetian castle, accessible by a winding road that coaches avoid
  • Fiskardo village on the north: survived the 1953 earthquake, intact Venetian architecture, best harbour on the island
  • Mt Ainos national park: ancient fir trees found nowhere else on earth, walk the summit ridge in complete solitude
