What the crowds actually look like

The trail and villages have a daily visitor cap but cruise excursion groups from Livorno and Genoa fill quota slots fast. Vernazza and Monterosso are the worst; Corniglia (no boat access) is the most manageable.

The five villages collectively receive ~2.5 million visitors a year in an area with fewer than 4,000 permanent residents. Monterosso and Vernazza are the most crowded. Corniglia (no ferry access) is the quietest. Entry fees and timed passes now required on the coastal trail in peak season.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Take the first morning train from La Spezia (6:30am) before day-tripper coaches arrive. Manarola at dusk after the crowds leave is exceptional.

Alternative: Portovenere — 20 min from La Spezia by ferry, same dramatic coastline, far fewer visitors, Byron's Grotto worth seeing.

Local intel

  • Corniglia is the only village with no boat access — most cruise groups skip it, dramatically quieter
  • Take the ferry between villages rather than the trail — trail requires a paid pass and gets extremely busy
  • Manarola for sunset after 5pm when the day-trippers leave — the harbour views are extraordinary and manageable
  • Portovenere is 20 min south and gets a fraction of the visitors — same colourful houses, no crowd quotas
