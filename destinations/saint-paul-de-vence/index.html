What the crowds actually look like

Medieval hilltop village that attracted Picasso, Matisse, Chagall, and Léger. Fondation Maeght (major modern art museum) is extraordinary. Gets crowded but remains genuinely beautiful.

Local intel

  • Fondation Maeght: one of the world's great private modern art collections, Miró sculptures in the garden — book ahead in summer
  • The village cemetery: Chagall is buried here in a simple grave — no cruise groups visit it
  • La Colombe d'Or hotel bar: Picasso paintings on the walls, historic celebrity haunt — you can have a drink
  • Vence old town (15 min walk down): Matisse's Chapelle du Rosaire is genuinely extraordinary and rarely crowded
