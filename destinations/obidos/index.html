What the crowds actually look like

Occasional Lisbon cruise excursion (1hr drive). One of Portugal's most beautiful small towns — intact medieval walls, ginja (sour cherry liqueur) served in chocolate cups. Manageable even on port days due to its size.

Local intel

  • Drink ginja from a chocolate cup at a stall on Rua Direita — the whole village does this
  • Walk the full circuit of the medieval walls — free, views of the surrounding valley, cruise groups rarely go all the way around
  • Visit on a weekday morning when coach groups haven't arrived
