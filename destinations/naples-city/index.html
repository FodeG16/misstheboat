What the crowds actually look like

Naples itself is a UNESCO historic centre with extraordinary art collections, street food, and Baroque churches. Cruise groups transit to Pompeii and the Amalfi Coast — fewer linger in the city.

Local intel

  • Pizza at Pizzeria Brandi (birthplace of Margherita pizza) or Di Matteo on Via Tribunali — the historic centre is the place for pizza
  • Museo Nazionale Archeologico: houses the greatest collection of Greco-Roman art in the world, the Farnese Bull and Pompeii erotic art room — rarely has long queues
  • Spaccanapoli (the street that splits Naples): the heart of the historic centre, genuinely overwhelming, best at 8am before the chaos begins
  • Cappella Sansevero: the Veiled Christ sculpture is one of the most astonishing works of art in Italy — small chapel, book ahead
  • Quartieri Spagnoli (Spanish Quarter): the gridded alleys, laundry strung between windows, shrines on every corner — the real Naples
