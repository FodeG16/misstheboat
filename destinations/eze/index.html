What the crowds actually look like

Villefranche cruise excursion staple — appears on most French Riviera shore excursion menus. The single road in means the village becomes genuinely congested when buses arrive 10am–2pm.

Tiny perched village (population ~400) receiving heavy day-tripper buses from Nice and Monaco. The village lane from the gate to the Jardin Exotique is genuinely impassable at peak times.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Before 9:30am or after 4pm. The exotic garden at the top is quieter than the main street even on busy days.

Alternative: Peillon — 30 min inland, equally dramatic perched village, not on any cruise itinerary, almost no tourists at all.
