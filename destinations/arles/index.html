What the crowds actually look like

Cruise excursion from Marseille (1hr). Roman amphitheatre, Van Gogh sites, and the Camargue are the draws. Less crowded than Avignon.

Local intel

  • Roman amphitheatre (Les Arènes) opens at 9am — first hour is almost empty
  • Van Gogh Foundation: not the hospital from the paintings (that's the Espace Van Gogh) but an extraordinary art space
  • Les Alyscamps (Roman necropolis) at dusk — one of the great atmospheric sites in Provence, almost never crowded
  • Camargue flamingo viewing at the Pont de Gau bird sanctuary — 15 min south, no cruise buses
