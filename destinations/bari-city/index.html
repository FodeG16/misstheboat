What the crowds actually look like

Gateway to Puglia and a cruise homeport. Bari Vecchia (old city) is an extraordinarily dense medieval labyrinth. Cruise groups transit to Alberobello and Matera — fewer explore the old city.

Local intel

  • Bari Vecchia early morning: elderly women make orecchiette pasta by hand in the doorways of Via Arco Basso — genuinely extraordinary, arrive before 10am
  • Basilica di San Nicola: one of Puglia's finest Romanesque churches, crypt with the bones of St Nicholas (the original Santa Claus)
  • Focaccia barese at Panificio Fiore: olive-oil-soaked focaccia bread, genuinely different from anywhere else in Italy
  • Lungomare seafront at sunset: locals' promenade, gelato, children playing — zero cruise groups this far from the port
