What the crowds actually look like

Rarely on cruise excursion menus — too far from Catania for a half-day. Ragusa Ibla (the lower town) is one of Sicily's best-preserved Baroque quarters with almost no cruise traffic.

Local intel

  • Ragusa Ibla: the lower town is an entirely intact Baroque quarter — walk down from Ragusa Superiore for a completely unjostled experience
  • Duomo di San Giorgio: the finest Baroque church facade in Sicily, huge steps, almost always quiet
  • Cioccolato di Modica: ancient Aztec-recipe chocolate (no emulsifiers, grainy texture) — buy from a producer in Modica 15 min east
