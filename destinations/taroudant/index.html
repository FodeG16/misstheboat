What the crowds actually look like

Walled market town 80km from Agadir, often called "Marrakech without the tourists." Intact red-ochre ramparts, traditional souks, and almost no cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Cycle the full circuit of the ramparts (7km) — hire a bicycle from the square, almost no other tourists do this
  • Souk des Bijoutiers (jewellery souk) for Berber silver — genuine craftsmen, no fixed prices, far less pressure than Marrakech
  • Riad lunch in the medina — several family-run riads serve set lunch to non-guests, extraordinary value
