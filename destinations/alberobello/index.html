What the crowds actually look like

UNESCO trulli district. Standard Bari cruise excursion. The Rione Monti district gets busy midday but the Aia Piccola neighborhood has almost no visitors.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Early morning before 9am or after 5pm.

Alternative: Locorotondo — 15 min away, circular hill town, whitewashed streets, almost no cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Aia Piccola neighborhood: 400 trulli, almost no tourist shops, real residents still living in them
  • Trullo Sovrano (the only two-story trullo, now a museum) is right in the main zone but usually manageable
  • Locorotondo 15 min away: circular hill town, extraordinary views over the Valle d'Itria, genuinely local
  • Cisternino village 20 min east: Pugliese town with almost no foreign tourists, outstanding street food (bombette pork rolls)
