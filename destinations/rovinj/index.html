What the crowds actually look like

One of the most beautiful towns on the Adriatic — pastel-coloured houses rising from the sea. Growing cruise stop. The old town peninsula can feel very crowded on summer port days.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Before 9am or after 6pm the old town is manageable.

Alternative: Motovun — hilltop truffle town in Istrian interior, 45 min inland, no cruise buses.

Local intel

  • St Euphemia church tower: climb it for panoramic views, cruise groups rarely make the effort
  • Batana boat house museum: traditional flat-bottomed fishing boat, extraordinary living cultural heritage, never crowded
  • Grisia Street art galleries: local artists display work on this steep cobbled street, far more interesting than souvenir shops
  • Motovun village in the Mirna Valley: truffle capital, hilltop walls, wine, entirely tourist-free outside weekends
