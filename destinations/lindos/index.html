What the crowds actually look like

Whitewashed village below a dramatic Doric acropolis. Rhodes cruise excursion staple — coach convoys arrive from 9:30am. The main street and acropolis path are extremely crowded midday.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Before 9am or after 4pm — the village becomes peaceful once the coaches leave.

Local intel

  • Acropolis at 8am opening: Temple of Athena and the Byzantine church ruins, dramatic views — arrive before the Rhodes coaches
  • Lindos from the beach (St Paul's Bay): looking up at the acropolis from the water is the best view and the beach is accessible by footpath
  • Village backstreets away from the donkey path: local houses with courtyard gardens, pebble-mosaic floors, no souvenir shops
