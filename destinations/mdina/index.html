What the crowds actually look like

Standard cruise excursion from Valletta. Small population, narrow streets. Very crowded during ship call windows but quiet outside them.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Early morning or late afternoon after buses leave.

Local intel

  • Mdina at 7am before the coaches arrive is as close to a medieval ghost town as Europe offers
  • Fontanella Tea Garden on the bastion walls — incredible views, locals know it but cruise groups rarely find it
  • Rabat village adjoining Mdina has the St. Paul's catacombs — almost no visitors even on port days
