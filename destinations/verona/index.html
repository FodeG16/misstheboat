What the crowds actually look like

Occasional Venice cruise excursion (2hr drive). The Arena, Piazza Bra, and Juliet's balcony are the cruise concentration points.

Beating the crowds

Timing: The Arena opera season (June–August) is spectacular but adds non-cruise crowds. Piazza delle Erbe early morning is excellent.

Local intel

  • Avoid Juliet's balcony — it's extremely crowded and entirely fictional. Castel San Pietro hill above the city has the real view
  • Piazza delle Erbe at 7am: the market set-up, locals buying vegetables, no tourists yet
  • Arena di Verona: even if not attending opera, the exterior at dusk is extraordinary and accessible
  • Giardino Giusti (Renaissance garden) is world-class and almost always manageable
  • Amarone wine tasting at an enoteca in the Valpolicella zone north of the city — the real stuff
