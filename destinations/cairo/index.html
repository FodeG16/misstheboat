What the crowds actually look like

Full-day cruise excursion from Alexandria (3hrs each way). The Giza Pyramids, Sphinx, and Egyptian Museum are the cruise stops. One of the world's great experiences but the logistics are significant — a full day barely scratches the surface.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Pyramids at dawn or dusk are dramatically less crowded than midday. The Sound & Light show is actually worthwhile.

Local intel

  • Solar Boat Museum next to the Great Pyramid: a 4,600-year-old intact wooden boat, rarely included in cruise tours
  • Egyptian Museum: the Tutankhamun galleries are extraordinary but arrive early — cruise groups fill them by 11am
  • Khan el-Khalili bazaar: the tourist section is the first alleyway — walk deeper for genuine workshops and merchants
  • Islamic Cairo (Al-Azhar area): the medieval mosque and madrasa complex is extraordinary and cruise tours almost never go
