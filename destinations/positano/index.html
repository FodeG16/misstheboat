What the crowds actually look like

Heavily photographed and heavily trafficked. Cruise excursion coaches from Naples and Salerno both route through. Access is bottlenecked by the single coastal road.

Day-trippers arrive by ferry from Naples, Salerno, and Amalfi from 10am onwards, compounding cruise excursion buses. The single main street and beach become impassable 11am–4pm in July and August. Population of 4,000 receives up to 10,000 visitors on peak days.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Ferry from Naples or Amalfi avoids road congestion. Before 9am the staircase streets are walkable.

Alternative: Praiano — 10 min west by ferry, half the people, same dramatic clifftop views, no cruise buses.

Local intel

  • Spiaggia Grande beach is the most crowded — walk 10 min to Fornillo beach instead
  • Take the SITA bus from Amalfi rather than a tour coach — local bus, local experience
  • The church of Santa Maria Assunta is free and usually quiet even when the town is packed
  • Leave by 2pm or stay until 6pm — the 2–5pm window is the absolute worst for crowds
