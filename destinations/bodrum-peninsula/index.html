What the crowds actually look like

The villages of the Bodrum peninsula (Yalıkavak, Türkbükü, Gündoğan) are the Turkish equivalents of St-Tropez — where Istanbul's wealthy summer. Less crowded than Bodrum town.

Local intel

  • Yalıkavak marina village: fishing boats and superyachts side by side, excellent fish restaurants on the water, no cruise buses
  • Gümüşlük village: wade to Rabbit Island across the causeway, submerged ancient city visible through clear water, local fish restaurants with tables in the sea
  • Karaincir and Bağla beaches on the south coast: completely undeveloped, crystal water, no beach clubs, accessible only by 4WD
