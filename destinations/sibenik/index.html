What the crowds actually look like

The Cathedral of St James (UNESCO) is one of the finest Renaissance buildings in the Adriatic and entirely built from stone with no mortar. Gateway to Krka waterfalls and Kornati islands. Growing cruise destination.

Local intel

  • Cathedral of St James: the frieze of 72 portrait heads of local citizens around the exterior is extraordinary — stand underneath it
  • Krka Waterfalls National Park: 20km north, stunning cascades — arrive early, coach tours fill the car park by 10am
  • Kornati Islands boat trip: 89 uninhabited limestone islands, extraordinary sailing, no cruise excursion reaches them without a private boat
  • Old fortress (Barone and St Anne): panoramic views over the channel, cruise groups cluster around the cathedral, far fewer climb up
