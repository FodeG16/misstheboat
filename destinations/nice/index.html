What the crowds actually look like

Major Riviera city — cruise ships tender from Villefranche 10km east. The Promenade des Anglais and Vieux-Nice are cruise focal points. City is large enough to absorb groups.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Cours Saleya flower market before 10am. The Russian Orthodox Cathedral area sees almost no cruise groups.

Local intel

  • Cours Saleya flower market at 7am before the tourist wave — buy socca (chickpea pancake) for breakfast
  • Colline du Château (Castle Hill) for panoramic views — free lift, cruise groups rarely make the climb
  • Musée Matisse and Musée National Marc Chagall are world-class and rarely crowded
  • Socca at Chez René Socca on Rue Miralheti — the best in Nice, locals-only queue before noon
  • Vieux-Nice back streets (Rue Pairolière) for shopping — local provisions, not tourist trinkets
