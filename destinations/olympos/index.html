What the crowds actually look like

Not on standard cruise menus — too far and too rugged for coach groups. The eternal flame (Chimaera) burning from the hillside is a genuine once-in-a-lifetime sight.

Local intel

  • Chimaera eternal flame: walk up at dusk, the natural methane flames are dramatic in low light — this is a truly remarkable natural phenomenon
  • Olympos ancient ruins: walk through a laurel forest to a Lycian/Roman city that is barely excavated, extraordinary atmosphere
  • Tree houses in Olympos village: rustic accommodation in wooden platforms in the trees, entirely non-touristy community
