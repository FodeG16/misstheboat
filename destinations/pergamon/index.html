What the crowds actually look like

Dramatic acropolis with the steepest ancient theatre in the world. Cruise excursion from Izmir. Far less crowded than Ephesus with equally impressive ruins.

Beating the crowds

Alternative: Ephesus via Kusadasi is 90 min the other direction — genuinely different sites worth comparing.

Local intel

  • The theatre is the steepest ancient theatre anywhere — 80-degree rake, 10,000 seats, extraordinary engineering
  • Red Basilica (Kizil Avlu) in town: vast 2nd-century Roman temple later converted to a Byzantine church — enormous and almost never crowded
  • Bergama market town below the acropolis: Tuesday market is purely local, excellent leather goods and produce
