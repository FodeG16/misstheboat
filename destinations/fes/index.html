What the crowds actually look like

The largest medieval Islamic city in the world — Fès el-Bali is genuinely disorienting. Long cruise excursion from Casablanca (3hrs) or Tangier (3hrs). Tour groups concentrate at the tanneries and main medina gates.

Beating the crowds

Timing: The tanneries from the leather shops above (usually free entry) are best in morning light.

Local intel

  • Tanneries viewed from above (enter the leather shops, they'll invite you up): morning light makes the colours extraordinary
  • The Bou Inania madrasa is more beautiful than the more-visited Attarine madrasa and less crowded
  • Hire a local guide (not a tour group guide) — the medina has 9,000 streets, you will get lost without one
  • Lunch at a rooftop restaurant overlooking the medina — the view changes everything
  • Rue Talaa Kebira from the Bab Bou Jeloud gate early morning: the main artery before the chaos begins
