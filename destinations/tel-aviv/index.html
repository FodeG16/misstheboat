What the crowds actually look like

35km from Ashdod port. Bauhaus White City (UNESCO), Carmel Market, and the beach. Cruise groups from Ashdod typically head to Jerusalem — Tel Aviv sees relatively few.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Carmel Market (Shuk HaCarmel) Friday morning is one of the great urban food experiences.

Local intel

  • Carmel Market on a Friday morning: enormous, chaotic, extraordinary — arrive before 11am
  • Bauhaus White City walking tour: the largest concentration of Bauhaus architecture in the world, free to walk
  • Jaffa Port and flea market — wander the Ottoman alleys, galleries in old warehouses, excellent hummus at Abu Hassan
  • Levinsky Market (spice market) in Florentin: saffron, dried fruit, pickles, halva — zero tourists
  • Hatachana (old train station complex): independent restaurants, galleries, local crowd at weekends
