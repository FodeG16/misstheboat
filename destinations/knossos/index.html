Beating the crowds

Local intel

  • Heraklion Archaeological Museum: houses all the best Knossos artefacts including the famous bull-leaping fresco — visit this before or instead of the site itself
  • Arrive at 8am opening: the site is walkable and atmospheric before the coach convoys arrive at 10am
  • The palace throne room: small, genuinely atmospheric — cruise groups crowd in and out but you can linger if you arrive early
