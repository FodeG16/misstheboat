What the crowds actually look like

Major Mediterranean cruise homeport and stop. The Cathedral (La Seu) and old town are cruise focal points. City is large enough to absorb groups.

Beating the crowds

Timing: La Seu Cathedral first thing in the morning. The Es Baluard museum area is rarely overrun.

Local intel

  • La Seu Cathedral: Antoni Gaudí restored the interior — extraordinary light through the rose window, arrive at 9am opening
  • Es Baluard (modern art museum): world-class collection in a converted bastion, almost always manageable
  • Mercat de l'Olivar: covered market, local produce, excellent tapas bar at the back — entirely Mallorcan clientele
  • Santa Catalina neighbourhood for lunch: the market hall and surrounding restaurants are local and excellent
  • Palau March museum: private collection including Rodin sculptures, Henry Moore, and a Miró tapestry — almost never crowded
