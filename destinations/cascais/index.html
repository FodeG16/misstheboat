What the crowds actually look like

40-minute train from Lisbon, not on standard cruise excursion menus. Genuinely local Atlantic resort town — historic centre, beaches, excellent seafood.

Local intel

  • Boca do Inferno (Hell's Mouth) cliffs at sunset — dramatic, free, few tourists on weekdays
  • Casa da Guia seafood restaurants above the cliffs — better than anything in the tourist centre
  • Bike the coastal path to Guincho beach — empty Atlantic beaches 10km from town
  • Museu Condes de Castro Guimarães is free, extraordinary building, virtually no visitors
