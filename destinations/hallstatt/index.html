What the crowds actually look like

Population of 780. The town now charges for car access and is trying to limit visitors. A Chinese developer literally built a full-scale replica of this village.

Up to 10,000 visitors on summer peak days in a town of 780 residents — one of the highest visitor-to-resident ratios in Europe. Buses from Salzburg (1.5h) and Vienna (3h) arrive continuously 10am–5pm.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Stay overnight — the experience after day-trippers leave at 6pm is completely different. Visit in November–March for snow and no crowds.

Alternative: Gmunden on Traunsee — less dramatic but genuinely lived-in Austrian lake town, no crowds, excellent.
