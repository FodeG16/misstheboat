What the crowds actually look like

Major Israeli cruise port. Bahai Gardens and Acre (Akko) are the standard excursions. City itself is relaxed and manageable.

Local intel

  • Bahai Gardens: the terraced gardens are extraordinary — arrive at the top and walk down for the best experience, cruise groups walk up
  • Acre (Akko) by train (30 min): one of the most complete Crusader cities in the world, UNESCO, far less visited than Jerusalem
  • German Colony neighbourhood in Haifa: tree-lined boulevard with excellent restaurants and cafés, almost no cruise groups
  • Wadi Nisnas neighbourhood: Arab-Jewish mixed quarter, street food, local market — a different Israel from the tour bus circuit
