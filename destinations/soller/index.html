What the crowds actually look like

Accessible by vintage tram from Palma — the train itself is a cruise excursion staple. The port (Port de Sóller) is more impacted than the town.

Local intel

  • Take the vintage wooden train from Palma: the experience is genuinely extraordinary — the cruise excursion version packs you in, but booking independently gives you flexibility
  • Fornalutx village 4km from Sóller: stone houses, mountain views, no coach parking — entirely peaceful
  • Orange and olive oil products from a local farm shop — genuine Mallorcan produce, not airport gifts
