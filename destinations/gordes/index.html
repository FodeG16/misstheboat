What the crowds actually look like

One of France's most beautiful villages — clinging to a cliff above the Luberon valley. Occasional Marseille cruise excursion. Gets crowded in summer but quiet on weekday mornings.

Beating the crowds

Timing: Before 9am or after 5pm.

Local intel

  • Abbaye de Sénanque (lavender-surrounded Cistercian abbey): June–July lavender blooms are extraordinary — arrive before 9am or after 5pm to avoid coaches
  • Village des Bories (prehistoric dry-stone hut settlement): 30 min walk from Gordes, extraordinary, almost never crowded
  • Drive the Luberon: Bonnieux, Lacoste (Marquis de Sade's castle), Ménerbes — all more peaceful than Gordes
