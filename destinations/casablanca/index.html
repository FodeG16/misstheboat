What the crowds actually look like

Atlantic cruise port and Morocco's largest city. Hassan II Mosque is the cruise focal point — the medina is less touristed than Marrakech but still busy on port days.

Local intel

  • Hassan II Mosque: the third-largest mosque in the world, guided tours available — genuinely extraordinary architecture
  • Old Medina: far less touristic than Marrakech, local shopping, real Casablanca life
  • Rick's Café: yes, it's themed around the film and the real Rick's didn't exist — but it's a beautiful space and excellent food
  • La Corniche seafront: locals' promenade, seafood restaurants, Atlantic views — not on any cruise itinerary
